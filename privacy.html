Privacy

Effective 22 November 2026.

擇角 (Zak Geok) runs in your browser. It has no account system and no application backend.

What stays on your device

The app stores current people, settings, saved profiles, locale, and a small geocoder cache in browser localStorage. Keys begin with hkfair.v2..

Share links put the current scenario in the URL fragment. Anyone who receives the link can see that scenario. Do not put sensitive information into people names or start places if you plan to share.

Clear site data in your browser to remove stored values.

External requests

The app sends place-search queries to Photon and requests vector map tiles from OpenFreeMap. Those providers may see your IP address and request metadata under their own policies. 擇角 (Zak Geok) does not receive those logs.

The app does not use analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking pixels. Icons are bundled locally.

Offline cache

A service worker caches app assets, local data files, and translation catalogues so the app can load again later. Clearing site data removes this cache.
